About the Interview

Getting to Know Kazakhstan

This interview was recorded as part of the SRAS language and cultural immersion programme. The goal was to hold a natural conversation of at least 10 minutes entirely in Russian, asking meaningful questions, listening carefully, and following up. The result is a window into a real person's life: their home, their culture, and their world.
